Altitude Rooftop Bar & Restaurant


Ambience

Perched high above the city skyline, Altitude offers a sweeping outdoor terrace with floor-to-ceiling views and a relaxed, upscale vibe. The mood is contemporary yet inviting, with soft ambient lighting, modern furnishings and a gentle breeze that complements the elevated setting. The city lights glimmer in the background, making it ideal for evening cocktails or a stylish dinner under the stars.



Menu & Drinks


Items

  • Starters & small plates: Think truffle-fries, char-grilled vegetables, tuna tataki, sharing boards.

  • Mains: Premium cuts of steak, fresh seafood, innovative vegetarian options and seasonal specials.

  • Signature drinks: Mixologist creations featuring local spirits, house-infused syrups and fresh herbs. Classic cocktails get a twist; there’s also an extensive selection of wines and craft beers.


Best Experience Highlights

  • Sunset to late-night: Arrive around sunset to catch the golden hour light and cityscape transition into night. Post-dinner, the venue often shifts into a more lounge-oriented atmosphere with mellow music, making it great for drinks and socialising.

  • Outdoor & indoor seating: While the terrace is the star, there are comfortable indoor lounge areas for cooler evenings or more private moments.

  • Dress code & crowd: Smart-casual works best—clean, stylish attire fits the setting. The crowd is a mix of locals celebrating special occasions and visitors seeking a memorable rooftop experience.


We are ideal For


  • Date nights : The view and ambiance set the stage for something special.
  • Celebrations :Birthdays, anniversaries or meeting friends—there’s a sense of occasion here.
  • Pre- or post-dinner drinks: Even if you opt for somewhere else for dinner, coming here for cocktails and the view is a solid choice.
